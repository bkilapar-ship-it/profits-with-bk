YouTube connection (needed once, for uploading)

Create it once at console.cloud.google.com: new project, enable YouTube Data API v3, OAuth consent screen set to External with your own Google account as a test user, then Credentials / Create client / Web application with https://bkilapar-ship-it.github.io as the authorised JavaScript origin. Paste the client ID here. When Google asks which account to use, pick the Oh, I Get It channel. The studio page uses the same ID, so it is shared.

Make the final video

Makes the finished video right here on your phone: the animation, your voice and your camera. No GitHub and no waiting for a build. Keep this page open and the screen on until it is done (a minute of video takes a few minutes). Then save it, share it, or upload it.

Uploading to YouTube needs a one-time Google setup in the ⚙️ Configuration tab.

Keep a copy (only when you are happy with the recordings)

Your recordings stay on this phone either way. These two only keep the episode file (words, prices, timings).

Thumbnails video and Shorts

A click-catching picture for the video (16:9) and for the Short (vertical). Your photo sits at the side and is cut out of its background. It uses your real photo as it is, with no changes to your face. Change the words, pick the side, or upload a different photo.

Make Shorts

A Short is vertical (9:16): the title on top, the scene pictures in the middle, big captions underneath, and your voice. Pick a suggested Short or tick your own scenes. Under 60 seconds works best. Make the full video first, or in any order: both use the same recordings.

How this works. Change any words, prices or timings in the scene cards and the preview updates. Each language has its own words and prices; the animation is shared. "Save to GitHub" updates the episode file, and the video build then makes one video per language. Lines in the "voiceover" boxes become the captions and your teleprompter script.
