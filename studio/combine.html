5. Layout

Side by side keeps the charts fully visible and puts you in a column next to them.

6. Create

It plays your recording in real time while it builds the video, so a 5 minute video takes about 5 minutes. Keep this page open and the screen on, and don't switch apps.

7. Thumbnail

The picture people see before they press play. Use your photo, take a selfie with an expression that fits the hook (serious, surprised, pointing), or pick a frame from your recording. Then save it and upload it with the video.
