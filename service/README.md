# Project 4: Service product (idea stage)

Goal: test whether people pay for finished animated explainer videos before building any software for sale.

## Offer to test (draft, not live)
- Customer sends a script (or topic) and, optionally, their own voice recording.
- They get an animated explainer, a vertical Short/Reel version, captions and a thumbnail.
- Audiences to try: small businesses, teachers and creators, financial-education channels.

## Cheapest first steps
1. Make 2 or 3 sample videos on non-financial topics as a portfolio.
2. Write a one-page offer with a fixed price per video; ask 5 people directly.
3. Only if people pay, turn the editor into a proper product (accounts, cleaner exports, no personal tokens).

## Cautions
- Paid finance content can count as a financial promotion in the UK: get advice before taking money for finance topics.
- Disclose sponsorships and affiliate links.
- Facts must be verified from original sources.
- Nothing here has been checked with a solicitor or accountant.
